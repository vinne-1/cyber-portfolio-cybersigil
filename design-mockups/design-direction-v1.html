Cybersigil Design Direction
Design Direction · Proposal for Review

One ornament language for the whole site — iconic, but never louder than the data.

This resolves everything pulled in so far — the Oxaley row-list layout, the Klimt gold-medallion ornament already live on the Home hero, and the texture/color-grading cues from Puparia and Mononoke — into one rule set, then shows it across three real page types: the hero, a content page (Capabilities), and a card grid (Work).

Nothing here touches the live site. It's a proposal to react to — mark up what to keep, cut, or push further.

RULE 01

Ornament fades, never sits under text

Every gold motif is a radial-gradient mask, same technique as the live hero medallion — full opacity at its own center, transparent well before it reaches a text column. No exceptions.

RULE 02

One iconic mark per page, not wallpaper

A page gets exactly one medallion-family ornament — large on the hero, a smaller corner version on content pages. It's a page's identity mark, not a repeating pattern.

RULE 03

Cards stay clean

Work/Products/Capability cards keep the existing blade-cut corner only. No gold texture inside a card — that's where the actual information lives.

RULE 04

Rows over boxes for lists

Oxaley's alternating-row pattern (already shipped on Work → Approach) is the default for anything list-shaped: capabilities, credentials, process steps.

RULE 05

Puparia/Mononoke = texture, not art

No character illustration, ever — both are copyrighted anime properties. What transfers: flat bold color blocking, mechanical/gear linework, muted base + one vivid accent.

RULE 06

Dark only

Single committed palette. No light-theme variants to design or maintain.

  1. Does the Capabilities corner-mark size read right, or is it fighting the heading — bigger, smaller, or drop it and keep gold to Home only?
