Approach

How the work gets done.

01

Manual Validation, Not Scanner Noise

Every finding is manually reproduced with Burp Suite, Nessus, and hands-on validation before it's reported — no raw scanner output passed through unchecked.

02

Risk-Mapped, PoC-Backed Reporting

Findings are mapped to OWASP Top 10 / OWASP API Top 10 and CVSS, documented with clear PoC, affected endpoints, and request-response evidence — not severity guesses.

03

Verified Closure, Not Just a Report

Every reported issue gets retest verification through closure, with false positives reduced in direct collaboration with DevOps/AppSec and risk explained in client walkthroughs.
